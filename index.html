Coach Buddy

Play first. Reflect later.

A friendly voice-and-character companion for League of Legends. It helps you scout before a game, cheers with you during it, and helps you learn from it afterwards.

How a game goes

  1. Loading screen

    Know your matchup

    Coach Buddy reads your lane opponent's public match history, such as the champion they play and their recent results. It also suggests a starter build plan based on the champions in the match.

  2. During the match

    Just good company

    It reacts to what you already see, like a kill or a dragon, with a cheer or a groan. No tips, no alerts, no analysis. Your game stays yours.

  3. After the match

    Reflect later

    A spoken and written review of your own game, with a few things to work on next time, so you can learn once the pressure is off.

What it never does

  • It never tracks enemy cooldowns, summoner spells or ultimates, and it shows no timers.
  • It never reveals information the game hides from you.
  • It never tells you what to do in a live game, and it sends no power-spike alerts.
  • It never presses keys, clicks or changes your game inputs for you.

Pick your coach

Choose a character and a voice. Every character is original to Coach Buddy, and you can change how each one looks.

  • MiroA playful chibi imp with horns, wings and a tail.
  • Mecha BuddyA floating galactic robot with a glowing visor.
  • AriaA blonde witch with a pointy hat and a magic staff.

Your data

What stays on your PC
Your settings, your chosen character and your Riot ID are kept on your own computer.
What is sent out
To make Coach Buddy talk, the text of a reply and any short recording you make while holding the talk key are sent to AI services that write and speak the answer. Match data comes from Riot's own services. You do not create an account, and no passwords are collected.
Cost
Coach Buddy is free to use. Optional paid extras that cost us money to run, such as smoother voice lines, may be added in the future. The free version will stay available.
